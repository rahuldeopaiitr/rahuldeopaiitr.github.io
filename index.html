About Me

PhD Research Scholar at IIT Roorkee working at the intersection of hydrology, flood hydrodynamics, water quality, and public-health risk — quantifying how urban floods transport contamination and translate into human exposure and infection risk.

§ 02 — Research

Research Themes

Hydro-Epidemiological Coupling

Linking flood hydrodynamics to flood-borne infection risk, so a rainfall event can be traced to a human-health outcome.

Urban Flood Hazard & Inundation

Hydrodynamic modelling of urban flooding in dense megacities, with spatio-temporal and geostatistical rainfall analysis.

Water Quality & QMRA

Modelling contaminant and pathogen fate in floodwater, translating exposure into infection probability via QMRA.

Climate & Socio-Economic Futures

Assessing how urbanisation, climate change and socio-economic dynamics reshape flood and health risk.
